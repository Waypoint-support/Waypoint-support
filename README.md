## Hi there 👋 
Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI roadmap generation work?

When you create a goal, Waypoint uses AI to generate a personalized step-by-step roadmap. Only your goal title, description, category, and timeline are sent to our AI provider. No personal identifiers or sensitive data are transmitted.

How do I create a new goal?

Tap the "+" button on the Goals screen or the "Create Goal" button on the Home screen. Enter your goal details and tap "Generate Roadmap" to get an AI-powered plan.

Can I edit my roadmap after it's generated?

Yes. After the AI generates your roadmap, you can review and adjust the waypoints and tasks before saving. You can also modify tasks at any time from the Goal Detail screen.

How does the XP and leveling system work?

You earn XP by completing tasks and hitting milestones. As you accumulate XP, you level up through navigator ranks. Streaks are tracked when you complete tasks on consecutive days.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your goals and progress are stored locally on your device. When generating roadmaps, only the goal details you enter are sent to our AI service — no personal identifiers, account data, or device information is shared. See our Privacy Policy in the app under Profile for full details.

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Profile and tap "Restore Purchases." This will restore any active subscriptions linked to your Apple ID.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete all your data from within the app. Go to Profile and look for the data management options.
